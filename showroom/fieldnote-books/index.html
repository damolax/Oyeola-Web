MORE THAN A BOOKSHOP

Stories bring people together.

Fieldnote is a made-up independent bookshop designed to show what great retail storytelling can do. Instead of simply listing inventory, the experience encourages discovery, invites readers to events and gives shoppers a reason to return.

For a real shop, the catalogue would connect to live stock, pickup or delivery, and the store's existing commerce system.
