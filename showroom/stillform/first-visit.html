First-timer questions

Before you arrive.

What should I wear?+

Comfortable clothes that let you move. Studios often ask for grip socks; check your real studio's policy before visiting.

What happens in Foundations?+

The example class focuses on the reformer, your breathing and simple controlled movements.

Can I start if I have an injury?+

If you are injured, pregnant, recovering or have health concerns, get advice from a qualified clinician before attending.

Can I book a place here?+

No. This sample site prepares an enquiry email; it does not reserve a studio seat.
