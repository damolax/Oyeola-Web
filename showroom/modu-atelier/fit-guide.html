Good furniture starts with good measurements.

Before you enquire

Know the space you have.

A few small checks now can save big questions later.

01

Measure your space.

Note the clear wall and floor measurements, not just the approximate room size. Leave room for walking and other furniture.

02

Measure the route.

Look at your front door, hallway, stairwells, lifts and tight turns. Record the narrowest clear opening, not the frame width.

03

Check with the maker.

Dimensions in this concept are examples. A real maker needs to confirm packaging, assembly and delivery access before an order.

Try the interactive fit helper.Open any product, select a width and enter your room and doorway dimensions. The on-page comparison will show what deserves a second look.
