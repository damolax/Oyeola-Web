Can I change the dimensions? +

The concept product pages include illustrative width options. In a real made-to-order business, a maker would confirm the structural and manufacturing possibilities.

Can I see fabric samples? +

You can choose up to four finish ideas and prepare a sample enquiry. No physical samples are shipped from this concept.

When will my furniture arrive? +

No production time or shipping date is displayed because this is an original concept, not an operating maker.
