Three competitor examples

What they already do well.

01 · Modern Animal

Services and nearby clinics.

Good: A prominent clinic finder, primary care, urgent care, and clear next steps. Our design difference: lead with four clear visit topics before asking for a location or app.

See competitor ↗
02 · Bond Vet

Primary and urgent care.

Good: Straightforward distinction between wellness needs and more urgent pet care. Our design difference: emergency guidance stays visible across every page, and the visitor understands why the sample form is not urgent care.

See competitor ↗
03 · Small Door Veterinary

Booking and helpful proof.

Good: Easy booking paths and detailed service and membership information. Our design difference: a first-visit guide and sample enquiry summary before asking a new pet parent to commit.

Three choices in Kindred Pet

Why the journey is simpler.

1. Start with a clear need.

The visit guide offers links to the service pages, not a fake veterinary diagnosis.

Open visit guide ↗

2. Keep urgent help separate.

Emergency guidance is present in the navigation and describes why a portfolio form must not be used.

See safety guidance ↗

3. Give a useful next step.

Visitors can prepare an enquiry, see a live summary, and download it. No real appointments are claimed.

Try the form ↗

This is an original concept brand, not a real veterinary clinic. The photos are illustrative. No team credentials, medical outcomes, booking availability or competitor conversion improvements are claimed. The advantages above are untested design ideas, not measured performance results.
