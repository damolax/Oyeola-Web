Designed for useful action

Why we built it this way.

1. Know where to start.

The first-visit page answers common questions and suggests a class based on experience and preferred pace.

Try class finder ↗

2. Find a suitable time.

Instead of scrolling through a huge timetable, visitors filter days, levels and morning/evening.

Try schedule filters ↗

3. Send a useful request.

The brief summarises class, level and preferences. Visitors can copy, download or email the result.

Try enquiry form ↗

Stillform is an original concept brand. It does not sell classes or claim real availability. These are UX hypotheses, not proven conversion gains. Full bookings require a genuine studio, calendar and payment provider.
