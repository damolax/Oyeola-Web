# Oyeola Online | Five real website builds
Created: 11 October 2026
Status: websites 01–04 built; website 05 planned

These are **full multi-page websites**, not isolated feature demos. Every new brand is an **original concept**, not a claim that its business exists or that the images show its completed client projects. Do not invent customers, reviews, awards, team credentials, real booking availability or sales results.

## Shared UX rules
- Every homepage: 4–5-word hero heading, introductory text under 10 words, a clear action and an image that shows the service.
- Other top-level sections: plain English and short text. About, Project Details, Service Details may be more thorough.
- Fast mobile navigation, keyboard-accessible controls, useful alt text, visible focus states, reduced-motion support, 44px touch targets.
- Every CTA has a useful next action, never a dead link. When payments, calendars, or email APIs are not connected, label the handoff clearly.
- Distinguish mockups, design concepts and client work. Do not claim higher conversion rates without analytics or experiments.
- Each site has at least: home, service details, project/product details, approach/about, and an enquiry/buying path.
- Put the UX review and competitor notes on Oyeola's showroom, not in the fictional brand's customer-facing pages.

## 01 | Arc & Acre | Outdoor living design
**BUILD: COMPLETE:** `/showroom/arc-and-acre/index.html` (six HTML pages, shared CSS and JavaScript).
**Brand:** Arc & Acre. Nature-led, quiet and confident. Not a real operating landscape firm.
**Colours:** forest #1A372B, ivory #FBF9F3, limestone #F3F0E8, leaf #365F48, clay #B96644, mint #DCE6D1.
**Fonts:** DM Serif Display for headings, DM Sans for body and controls. H1 clamp(68px,7.3vw,120px) desktop, 59–83px mobile. H2 clamp(43px,5vw,70px). Body 16px, labels 10–12px.
**Hero:** "Life grows outside." / "Gardens made for how you live."
**Pages:** Home; Projects (category filters); Project details (six concept studies); Services (garden, pool & terrace, courtyard); Approach; Start Project (working brief builder).
**Real user actions:** Filter and open project cards; navigate among 6 pages; choose a space/style/budget/timeline; see a live brief; copy, download, or open a prefilled email to Oyeola Online. Email handoff requires the visitor to press Send. No fake payment or actual booking.
**Business goal:** help a high-intent visitor find the right design service and contact the studio with a useful brief.
**Competitor benchmark:**
1. Yardzen — https://yardzen.com/ — does well: clear packages and a planning quiz. Our testable improvement: an earlier direct link from visual inspiration to a lightweight brief, with no package-choice wall.
2. Bower & Branch Design Studio — https://bowerandbranch.com/pages/design-studio — does well: professional regional advice and a clear three-step process. Our improvement: a faster first decision between garden, pool and small courtyard in fewer words.
3. ShrubHub — https://www.shrubhub.com/ — does well: 3D visual proof, packages and up-front pricing. Our alternative: fewer competing sale messages and an editorial, image-led path for premium project enquiries.
**Why it may convert better for the chosen audience:** fewer steps to a useful enquiry, visible design examples, transparent concept status and form summary. This is a UX hypothesis, not a measured result.
**Truth & media:** photo illustrations are credited to Unsplash, no real Arc & Acre client projects are claimed.

## 02 | Stillform | Reformer Pilates studio
**Status: BUILT AND PUBLISHED.** Main URL: `/showroom/stillform/index.html`. 11 main/detail HTML pages, stylesheet, and JavaScript.
**Brand:** warm modern wellness; soft movement, clear structure.
**Colours:** deep blue-green #203A40; cream #F7F1E9; peach #E4B6A7; seafoam #D5E5DE; ink #19272B.
**Fonts:** Fraunces (display, H1 clamp(67px,7.15vw,117px) desktop/59–77px mobile, H2 43–78px); Manrope (body 16px, buttons 11–12px, labels 10–11px).
**Hero:** "Move how you feel." / "Strong movement. Calm mind. Your kind of pace."
**Features implemented:** four class-detail pages; filterable example timetable (day, level, morning/evening); first-class chooser; starter guide; clear membership pathways without invented prices; enquiry form with live brief, copy/download and transparent mailto handoff. No fake live slots, checkouts, trainers or medical claims.
**Conversion goal:** qualified first-class enquiry with clear class level and chosen time; live booking would require a real studio calendar.
**Competitors:**
1. Elroisè Wellness Center — https://www.elroisewellnesscenter.com/ — visible Pilates service lengths, book-now buttons and prices. Our proposed difference: dedicate the entire site to clear Pilates class levels and a first-visit route.
2. BR Reformer Studios — https://brreformerstudios.com/lagos-membership/ — clear starter routes and membership choices. Our proposed difference: show class and time filters before a long membership decision.
3. Sage & She — https://www.sageandshewellness.com/pricing — clear drop-in/class packs and varied class types. Our proposed difference: a short "Which class fits me?" tool that sends users straight to suitable class details.
**Why designed to help:** fewer decisions before an enquiry; visible class difficulty; real class detail pages; relevant filtered example timetable; no unsupported prices or booking claims. This is an unmeasured UX hypothesis. Measure click-through and enquiry completion with a real studio analytics setup.

## 03 | Kindred Pet | Veterinary care
**Status: BUILT AND PUBLISHED.** Main URL: `/showroom/kindred-pet/index.html`. 11 HTML content pages, a responsive stylesheet, shared JS interaction and favicon.
**Brand:** warm, trustworthy and clinical without being cold.
**Colours:** dark teal #184C4A, pale linen #F7F5ED, warm coral #E79C81, light sage #DDE9DD.
**Fonts:** Lora (headings, H1 desktop clamp(65px,7.2vw,113px), mobile clamp(60px,14vw,78px); H2 responsive 42–71px). Sora (body 15–16px, UI 10–13px).
**Hero:** "Care for every paw." / "Thoughtful pet care, made easier to understand."
**Implemented features:** care service directory and 4 in-depth service detail pages; visit guide for cats and dogs that only points to information pages (never diagnosis/triage); separate emergency guidance with external generic map search; first-visit checklist; about page; sample enquiry builder with live summary, copy, download and mailto to Oyeola Online. No real veterinarians, clinic, location, pricing, patient records or appointment availability are claimed. All pages disclose that the brand is an original portfolio concept.
**Conversion goal:** understandable service choice and qualified *sample enquiry*; a real care appointment would require connected veterinary staff, clinic details, consent/privacy workflows and a verified booking provider.
**Competitors:**
1. Modern Animal — https://www.modernanimal.com/ — booking and nearby-clinic search. Improve by showing typical appointment purpose and preparation before asking a visitor to download an app.
2. Bond Vet — https://bondvet.com/ — primary and urgent care paths. Improve by making these paths visually distinct in one first screen.
3. Small Door Veterinary — https://www.smalldoorvet.com/ — quality proof and easy booking. Improve with upfront next-step guidance for first-time pet parents.
**Why designed to help:** a top-level emergency route, unambiguous service paths, no false staff credibility, and a working first-enquiry summary. This remains a UX hypothesis with no measured conversion results.

## 04 | Modu Atelier | Made-to-order furniture
**Status: BUILT AND PUBLISHED.** Main URL: `/showroom/modu-atelier/index.html`. Fourteen full pages: home, searchable/filterable collection, six dedicated product pages, materials and four-swatch picker, room measurement guide, studio approach, about, locally saved configurations and functional design enquiry. Includes responsive CSS and JavaScript.
**Brand:** sculptural, calm, crafted.
**Colours:** charcoal #29241F, chalk #F7F4EC, sienna #AA5B3F, clay #D6C7B8, cream #EDE9E0.
**Fonts:** Cormorant Garamond (H1 84px/H2 54px), DM Sans (body 16px, controls 13px).
**Hero:** "Furniture made to fit." / "Choose the shape, size and finish."
**Features implemented:** product-category and name filters, six detail pages with selectable finishes, widths and feet, a first-pass room/doorway measurement checker, save/remove shortlist in browser storage, select up to four sample colours, an enquiry form prefilling the chosen configuration and finish samples, live summary and copy/download/mailto handoff. No prices, checkout, live inventory or manufacturing delivery promises because no supplier is connected.
**Conversion goal:** a confident fabric sample or custom furniture enquiry.
**Competitors:**
1. Interior Define — https://swatches.interiordefine.com/ — exceptional sampling and fabric filters. Improve by putting dimension-fit guidance immediately after picking a fabric.
2. BenchMade Modern — https://benchmademodern.com/collections/sofas — structured customization and concierge guidance. Improve with a quick "Will this fit?" check before selection fatigue.
3. Joybird — https://joybird.com/samples/ — free swatch kit and product personalization. Improve by keeping the chosen swatch, measurements and saved design in one consistent summary.
**Why designed to help:** customers can bring piece, size, finish and room context together rather than beginning with a vague request. Comparable competitors offer swatches, sizing and customisation; Modu deliberately keeps them in a single short enquiry path. These are design intentions, not measured conversion results.
