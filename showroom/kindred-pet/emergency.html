Important emergency information

For urgent pet care.

If your pet may be in danger, contact a real emergency vet.

Do not wait for this website

Seek veterinary help now.

This website cannot assess, treat or refer emergencies. Trouble breathing, collapse, seizures, serious injuries, uncontrolled bleeding and suspected poisoning may require immediate veterinary attention. Call a nearby 24-hour emergency veterinary hospital or go there now, following professional advice. If your pet may have swallowed poison, tell the emergency vet what you suspect.
