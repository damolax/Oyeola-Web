First-visit questions

Good to know.

When should I make the appointment?+

Contact a real clinic to discuss timing based on your pet's age and needs.

Do I need pet records?+

Bring anything you have. A clinic can explain what else it needs.

Can I book on this website?+

No. This is a portfolio concept. The sample form opens an email to Oyeola Online.

What if my pet needs help right now?+

Do not wait for an online form. Contact an emergency veterinary service in your area immediately if there are serious warning signs.
