Common questions

What to know.

Can this site assess my pet's symptoms?+

No. This website offers no diagnosis, triage or medical advice.

My pet looks seriously unwell. What now?+

Do not use this form. Contact a local emergency vet immediately.

Can I get an urgent appointment here?+

No. The sample form cannot contact a vet or hold a real appointment.
