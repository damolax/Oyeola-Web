Oyeola / About

The guide, not the hero

Good businesses should not lose opportunities because the experience around them is harder than it needs to be.

Oyeola is built around one practical idea: understand the friction first, then build the smallest complete thing that genuinely improves it.

How I work

I am not attached to the tool.

A platform can be useful and still be the wrong recommendation. A website can be old and still not need a redesign. A planner can have fewer pages and still be a better product.

  • Research before prescription. Understand the real experience first.
  • Evidence before confidence. Use working examples and explainable diagnostics.
  • Priorities before scope. Separate “do first” from “do later.”
  • Use before decoration. Design should make the next step clearer.

The plan
