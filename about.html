The story behind Oyeola

Why I Built Oyeola Web

My name is Olalekan Oyekunle. You may also know me as Os Oyeola. I created the name Oyeola by combining parts of my first and last names, Olalekan and Oyekunle. This is personal to me. My name is behind the work.

Before I had this website, I built my freelance business on Fiverr. I worked with more than 70 people through the platform, alongside projects I handled independently and offline. Those experiences taught me what clients really need: someone who listens, takes their goals seriously and delivers work they can actually use.

Then I was taken off Fiverr. I was furious. I had put real time and energy into building something there, and suddenly I could no longer rely on it. I won't pretend that was easy.

But the frustration gave me a new kind of determination. I knew I had to build my own place, find my own clients and keep serving them with the best work I could offer. I didn't want my future to depend entirely on another platform.

That is why Oyeola Web exists. This is where I can show you what I've built, explain how I approach a problem and talk with you directly. Whether it's a website, an online store or an automation system, I want to make something that genuinely helps your business.

And the story isn't finished. If you have an idea, a problem or a project in mind, we can keep talking.
