Your guide

You should not have to buy a tool before anyone understands the problem.

I help businesses and digital product sellers decide what actually needs to change, then I build the website, operating system or planner product around that outcome.

Why I work this way

The wrong solution can make a real problem more expensive.

A business can buy Airtable when the real issue is unclear ownership. A website can be redesigned when the real issue is a weak offer. A planner can add 300 pages when the real issue is that buyers cannot tell who it is for.

My job is to help you avoid that.

  • I start with what you want to improve. More customer action, less manual coordination or a stronger product.
  • I show you the problem in practical terms. What is unclear, duplicated, missing or slowing the decision down.
  • I recommend the smallest complete fix. Keep, connect, improve, rebuild or automate only where it earns its place.
  • I build for real use. The finished work has to function for customers, teams or buyers—not just look polished in a screenshot.
