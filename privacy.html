Privacy policy

Your Information, Handled Clearly.

This policy explains what Oyeola Online may collect when you use this website, why it is used and the choices available to you.

At a glance

Privacy without the mystery.

I collect only information that is useful for responding to enquiries, understanding projects and operating this website. I do not sell your personal information.

Privacy questions ↗

1. Information you choose to provide

When you contact Oyeola Online or complete a project, diagnostic or planner form, you may provide details such as your name, email address, company or website URL, project requirements, budget information, files and any other information you choose to include.

2. How that information is used

Your information may be used to reply to your enquiry, assess project fit, prepare a proposal or recommendation, deliver requested work, provide support, maintain normal business records and improve the clarity of the services offered on this website.

3. Website and technical information

Like most websites, hosting and infrastructure providers may process basic technical information needed to deliver the site securely, such as IP address, browser type, device information, requested pages, timestamps and security logs.

4. Forms and service providers

Website submissions may be stored in Oyeola Online's database or routed through a form-delivery provider when an email fallback is required. Hosting, database, email and similar service providers may process information only as needed to provide those services.

5. External content and links

This website may load fonts, images or other resources from third-party services and may link to external websites such as Instagram, LinkedIn or project demos. Those services operate under their own privacy policies and may receive technical request information when their content is loaded or when you follow a link.

6. Cookies and analytics

Oyeola Online does not currently use advertising cookies or a third-party behavioural advertising system. The site may use essential browser storage for features such as remembering your light or dark theme preference. If analytics or additional cookie-based services are added later, this policy will be updated.

7. How long information is kept

Information is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for an enquiry, active project, support, legal obligations or ordinary business record keeping. Information that is no longer needed may be deleted or anonymised.

8. Your choices

You may request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information you have submitted, subject to any information that must reasonably be retained for legal, contractual or record-keeping purposes.

9. Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect information handled through the website. No internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so sensitive passwords or payment-card details should not be sent through ordinary contact forms.

10. Contact

For a privacy question or request, email hello@oyeolaonline.com.
