Oyeola / Privacy

Privacy

Your details are used to deliver what you asked for and respond to your enquiry.

Oyeola does not sell submitted contact information.

What is collected

When you submit a form, Oyeola may collect your name, email address, website or shop link, project interest, message, the page you submitted from and the submission time.

Why it is collected

The information is used to deliver requested resources, respond to project enquiries, understand which Oyeola service you are interested in and maintain a record of relevant business conversations.

Where it is processed

Lead records are designed to be stored in Supabase. Transactional emails are designed to be sent through the configured email delivery provider. Hosting and serverless processing are provided through the website hosting platform.

How long it is kept

Business enquiry records may be retained while they remain relevant to the enquiry, project relationship or reasonable business record-keeping needs. You can request deletion.

Your choices

You can request access, correction or deletion of information you submitted by emailing oyeolawebmaster@gmail.com.
