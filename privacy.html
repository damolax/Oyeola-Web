Privacy notice

What happens to information you submit here.

This notice covers project enquiries, Website Check and Operations Check follow-up, and planner-resource access.

Information collected

Depending on the form, Oyeola may collect your name, email address, website or business URL, service interest, timeline, the problem you describe, the result you want, and diagnostic scores or answers you choose to submit.

How it is used

The information is used to respond to your request, understand the context before recommending work, provide requested planner resources, and improve which parts of the website are useful to visitors.

Where it is stored

Form submissions may be stored in Oyeola's Supabase database. Email delivery may be handled by an email service configured for the website. Oyeola does not sell submitted contact information.

How long it is kept

Enquiry information is kept only as long as reasonably useful for responding to the enquiry, maintaining project history, or meeting normal business record-keeping needs.

Your choices

You can ask for submitted information to be corrected or deleted by emailing oyeolawebmaster@gmail.com.

Website analytics

No third-party analytics account is currently connected. If analytics is added later, this notice should be updated to explain what is measured and which provider is used.
