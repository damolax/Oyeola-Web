The principle

The question is not “What feature can we add?”

The better question is: what does this business's customer struggle to understand, picture, configure or decide before they buy?

01

Start with the problem

Every demo begins with one friction point in the customer's buying journey.

02

Make the website respond

The customer changes something and the experience changes meaningfully in return.

03

Carry it into conversion

The chosen configuration, layout, menu, look or setup can become the next booking, quote or cart action.
