An ecommerce experience designed to connect product discovery with the meaning and story behind the brand.

Project context

The store needed to sell products without losing the story and values that make the brand distinctive, while still giving shoppers a practical route through the catalog.

The friction

When storytelling and shopping live in separate experiences, visitors are forced to choose between understanding the brand and using the store.

Key design decisions

  • Integrate the brand story into the shopping journey without blocking product discovery.
  • Keep product categories and purchase actions practical.
  • Use meaning and product context together at the decision point.

What changed in the journey

Shoppers can understand the brand context, discover products and continue toward purchase without choosing between the story and the store.
