The story behind the build

The brand story and the store needed to strengthen each other.

3 Nails 1 Cross is a mission-led ecommerce project, so the website could not behave as if the story and the products were two unrelated things.

I used Wix to keep shopping practical through clear categories and predictable purchase actions, while giving the brand meaning enough presence to explain why the products exist.

The finished experience lets shoppers understand the mission and move naturally into product discovery without one side slowing down the other.
