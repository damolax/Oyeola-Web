Latonya Speaks

A speaker-led brand experience designed to make the person, message and engagement path easier to grasp.

Project context

The website needed to establish the speaker's identity and message quickly while giving organizers and interested visitors a clear route to learn more or discuss an engagement.

The friction

Speaker sites can become a collection of biography, topics and media without making it obvious what an organizer should do next.

Key design decisions

  • Keep the speaker and core message central.
  • Separate engagement information from general brand storytelling.
  • Give organizers a clear enquiry path with enough context before contact.

What changed in the journey

Visitors can understand who the speaker is, what the message is about and how to move toward an engagement in one coherent journey.
