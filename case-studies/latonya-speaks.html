The story behind the build

A speaker website has only a few seconds to make the person, message and booking path clear.

Latonya Speaks needed to answer the questions an event organiser asks quickly: who is this speaker, what is the message, and how do I bring her into an event?

I built the story around that sequence instead of burying the important information inside a long biography. The speaker stays central, the message has its own space, and practical engagement information is easy to find.

The result is a personal-brand experience that supports both quick scanning and deeper reading while keeping the enquiry path obvious.
