The story

What the project was really solving.

The design was built around that sequence instead of asking organizers to hunt through a biography for the information they need.

The speaker stays central, the message is separated from engagement details, and the enquiry route is treated as part of the main story rather than something hidden at the bottom of the site.

The challenge

Make the person, message and engagement path clear quickly.

What I changed

  • Keep the speaker and credibility central.
  • Separate the message from practical engagement information.
  • Make enquiry easy for organizers.
  • Use a clear hierarchy that works for both quick scanning and deeper reading.

Where it ended

Visitors can understand the speaker's positioning and move toward an engagement without searching for the next step.
