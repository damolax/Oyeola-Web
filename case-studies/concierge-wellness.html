The story behind the build

More services should create more ways to help, not more confusion.

Concierge Wellness & Consulting had several services that needed to live together. The challenge was making those choices feel simpler for the person arriving on the site, rather than forcing them to understand the whole business before knowing where they belonged.

I grouped related services into clearer paths, explained the differences before the contact decision and placed trust close to the moments where a visitor would naturally hesitate.

The finished journey keeps the calmer tone expected from a wellness brand while giving people a clearer route to the service that fits them.
