The story

What the project was really solving.

The challenge was not a lack of services. It was the opposite: several paths had to live together without making visitors sort through everything before knowing where they belonged. I organized the experience so related services could be understood in groups, the differences between options were easier to see, and trust appeared close to the point where a visitor would decide to make contact.

The challenge

Present multiple wellness and consulting services without overwhelming the visitor.

What I changed

  • Group related services into clearer paths.
  • Explain the difference between options before asking for contact.
  • Place trust and credibility close to decision points.
  • Keep the page calm enough for a wellness audience while maintaining clear actions.

Where it ended

Visitors can identify the service that is most relevant to them and move toward contact with less uncertainty.
