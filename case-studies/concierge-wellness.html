Concierge Wellness & Consulting

A multi-service wellness site organized so visitors can identify the kind of help that fits them without reading the entire business offer.

Project context

The website needed to present several wellness and consulting services while helping visitors understand where they fit instead of facing one long undifferentiated list.

The friction

When several services sit at the same visual level, visitors have to understand the whole business before choosing the part that is relevant to them.

Key design decisions

  • Group related services into clearer customer paths.
  • Use supporting explanation only where it helps a visitor distinguish options.
  • Place trust and contact cues close to the service decision.

What changed in the journey

Visitors can scan the available help, identify a relevant service path and move toward contact with less interpretation.
