The story

What the project was really solving.

Bare Canvas Media needed room for a stronger visual identity, but design could not become decoration for its own sake. I used Wix Studio to create a clearer hierarchy between the main offer, supporting services and the next step. The visual system could stay expressive because the information architecture underneath it remained disciplined.

Skills used

The challenge

Keep a strong visual identity while making the agency offer easy to scan and understand.

What I changed

  • Use design to reinforce hierarchy instead of compete with it.
  • Separate the primary offer from supporting services.
  • Keep important actions obvious across the layout.
  • Build responsive behavior intentionally rather than simply shrinking desktop sections.

Where it ended

The site feels distinctive, but a visitor can still understand what the agency offers and where to go next without working for it.
