The story

The job was to make a broad range feel easier to navigate.

VapourVend needed the product range to feel broad without feeling chaotic. The design work focused on discovery: stronger categories, consistent browsing patterns and purchase actions that behave the same way wherever a customer is in the catalogue.

The point was not to reduce the number of choices. It was to reduce the effort required to understand those choices.

The challenge

What I changed

  • Kept category behavior consistent across the store.

Where it ended

Shoppers can move through the range with less friction and less uncertainty about where to go next.
