Real project · Ecommerce website

VapourVend

An ecommerce presentation built to make a broad product range easier to browse and the purchase path easier to follow.

Project context

The store needed a clearer way to present its range so shoppers could move through product choices without the catalog feeling like one undifferentiated list.

The friction

Large catalogs create choice overload when categories, product differences and next actions do not give shoppers enough orientation.

Key design decisions

  • Strengthen product discovery before the individual product page.
  • Use a consistent route from browsing into product-level decisions.
  • Keep purchase actions predictable across the range.

What changed in the journey

Shoppers can move through the range, reach relevant product information and continue toward purchase with a clearer sense of where they are.
