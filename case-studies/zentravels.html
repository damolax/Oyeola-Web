The story

What the project was really solving.

ZenTravels needed both sides of the journey: the emotional pull of travel and a practical route into planning. I organized the experience around travel intent, kept useful resources tied to the trip itself and made the next planning action obvious enough that visitors did not lose momentum after browsing.

The challenge

Turn travel inspiration into a clearer planning path.

What I changed

  • Organize content around travel intent.
  • Tie resources back to the trip instead of scattering them.
  • Keep the next planning step visible.
  • Use imagery to inspire without letting it replace useful information.

Where it ended

Visitors can move from inspiration toward a practical trip enquiry without losing the thread of the experience.
