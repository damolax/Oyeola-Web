The story

What the project was really solving.

The owner already understood websites. What she did not have was the time to turn the apartment into the kind of direct-booking experience she wanted.

Her booking operation used Smoobu, with Smoobu connected to Airbnb. Airbnb brought reach, but the fees made direct booking more important. The website therefore had a very practical job: give guests enough confidence to book the stay directly instead of treating the site as another brochure.

I built the experience in Wix around the questions a holiday guest naturally asks: What does the apartment feel like? What is included? What is nearby? What do previous guests say? And where do I check my dates?

The challenge

The site had to sell the stay visually, make location and amenities easy to understand, and lead naturally into direct availability without feeling like a generic rental listing.

What I changed

  • Lead with the stay experience, not technical booking language.
  • Bring guest capacity, bedrooms, bathrooms and parking into the decision early.
  • Use amenities, guest feedback and local-area information to build confidence before the availability CTA.
  • Keep the Smoobu-led booking journey connected to the website instead of making visitors start over elsewhere.

Where it ended

The end point was a direct-booking website that felt much more considered than a simple Wix build. The client, despite being a website designer herself, was wowed by what could be achieved with Wix when the experience was designed around the guest journey.
