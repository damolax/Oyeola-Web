The story

The website was not the first problem. The booking model was.

The owner already understood websites. What she did not have was the time to turn the apartment into the kind of direct-booking experience she wanted.

Her booking operation used Smoobu, with Smoobu connected to Airbnb. Airbnb brought reach, but the fees made direct booking more important. So the website needed to do a very practical job: give guests enough confidence to book the stay directly instead of treating the site as another brochure.

I built the experience in Wix around the questions a holiday guest naturally asks: What does the apartment feel like? What is included? What is nearby? What do previous guests say? And where do I check my dates?

The challenge

Sell the stay, explain the area and move people into direct availability without making the site feel like a generic property listing.

What I changed

  • Led with the stay experience instead of technical booking language.
  • Brought guest capacity, bedrooms, bathrooms and parking into the decision early.
  • Used amenities, guest feedback and nearby locations to build confidence before the availability action.
  • Kept the Smoobu-led booking journey connected to the website so visitors did not have to restart their decision elsewhere.

Where it ended

The result was a direct-booking site that felt far more considered than a basic Wix build. The client, despite being a website designer herself, was wowed by what I was able to create with Wix.
