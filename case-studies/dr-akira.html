Real project · Mental-health service website

Dr. Akira

A professional service website structured to present authority, services and appointment options in a calmer sequence.

Project context

The website needed to establish professional credibility while making sensitive service information and the route to an appointment straightforward for prospective clients.

The friction

Visitors in a sensitive service context need clarity and reassurance without being overloaded with information or pushed into an appointment before they understand the service.

Key design decisions

  • Establish the professional identity and trust cues early.
  • Separate service explanation from appointment action so visitors can orient themselves first.
  • Keep the appointment path visible without making it the only way to navigate.

What changed in the journey

Prospective clients can understand who provides the service, review relevant help and find the appointment path without searching across disconnected sections.
