The story

What the project was really solving.

This project needed to balance professional authority with a calmer, more approachable path into services and appointments. The structure gives visitors space to understand the expertise and the service before they are asked to act. Booking remains visible, but the page earns that action through context and trust first.

PlatformWordPress

Skills used

WordPressHealthcare UXService ArchitectureBooking JourneyTrust DesignResponsive Design

The challenge

Present professional authority, services and appointments in a calm sequence.

What I changed

  • Show professional trust early without making the page feel clinical or cold.
  • Explain services before asking for an appointment.
  • Keep the booking path visible throughout the experience.
  • Use a clear content sequence so visitors can move at their own pace.

Where it ended

Prospective clients can understand the service, build confidence and then choose an appointment path with more context.
