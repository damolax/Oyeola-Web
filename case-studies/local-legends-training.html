01 / The challenge

Before the design, there was a decision to make.

A parent can find hundreds of coaches promising better skills. What is harder to find is a clear sense of how that coach trains, what their standards are and where a young player belongs. The website needed to communicate the difference before someone committed their time.

02 / The thinking

What the website needed to say.

Local Legends is built around a demanding but simple belief: progress comes from consistency, structure and implementation. Instead of treating those ideas as another paragraph of marketing copy, the homepage turns them into the central story. Visitors encounter the three pillars before they are asked to choose a programme.

The founder’s story follows naturally. Training is personal, and parents need to know whose approach they are buying into. A dedicated founder section connects the programme’s standards to the experience behind them, with photography, a clear mission and space for the coach’s own voice.

Only after that context does the design introduce the choice: an Elite Membership for a regular training routine or Flexible Sessions for families looking for a different commitment level. That order matters. It puts meaning before pricing and turns an application into a more informed decision.

03 / The design decisions

Every section has a reason to be there.

Clearer programme fit before an application.

01

Lead with a standard, not a sales pitch.

The first impression is bold and focused. The headline says who the programme is for, then the blue application button gives motivated visitors somewhere to go.

02

Make the method easy to remember.

Consistency, Structure and Implementation are treated as three visible pillars, not buried in a long list of services.

03

Give parents reasons to trust the process.

The founder story, programme choices and parent/athlete feedback appear before the final application call to action.

04

End with one decisive next step.

A full-width application section makes it clear what committed athletes and families should do next.

The training philosophy

Three ideas. One clear standard.

Rather than present a long list of drills, the website gives families three easy ideas to remember. They explain how the programme approaches real development.

The finished experience

The finished WordPress experience presents one consistent message from the hero to the final application: serious development takes a serious commitment. The layout helps parents and athletes understand the method, see the programme options and decide whether to apply.

A purposeful customer journey is a foundation for stronger enquiries and conversions. Individual performance figures are not presented for this project.
