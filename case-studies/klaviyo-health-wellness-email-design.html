What was designed

Six emails. One consistent brand experience.

Each template serves a specific communication need while sharing the same visual language, mobile-first structure and clear calls to action.

01 / Promotional email

Promotion

A focused offer with clear visual priority and a direct path to the next action.

02 / Product email

Recommendations

Product imagery and supporting information organized to make discovery feel effortless.

03 / Trust email

Customer reviews

Customer feedback presented with stronger readability and a coherent brand treatment.

04 / Transactional email

Order confirmation

Essential purchase information structured for clarity and easy reading on mobile.

05 / Relationship email

Re-engagement

A thoughtful reminder that reconnects the brand with returning shoppers.

06 / Offer email

Discount offer

Promotional details presented without sacrificing hierarchy or brand consistency.

Design decisions

Built for recognition and readability.

The project used an emerald, cream and gold direction to connect the email designs visually. The layouts balance product imagery, concise copy, distinct sections and clear CTA placement.

Mobile reading was considered throughout, with coordinated typography and spacing across all six templates.

  • One consistent visual system across six email purposes.
  • Organized content blocks for desktop and mobile viewing.
  • Clear distinction between promotions, information and customer follow-up.
