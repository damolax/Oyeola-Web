Shop & Ship Express Analytics

A reporting experience designed to make website traffic, clicks, pages and performance signals easier to inspect together.

Project context

The business needed a clearer way to review website activity and performance signals without treating each metric as an isolated number.

The friction

Raw metrics are difficult to act on when the decision-maker must manually connect traffic, click and page information before seeing where attention is needed.

Key design decisions

  • Bring the important website signals into one consistent view.
  • Group related metrics so comparison is easier.
  • Use the reporting layer to surface questions and exceptions rather than decorate the dashboard.

What changed in the journey

A decision-maker can inspect the main website signals from one place and identify where deeper analysis or a site change may be needed.
