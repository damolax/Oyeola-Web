The story

What the project was really solving.

The project was less about putting more numbers on a screen and more about bringing related website signals into one operating view. I grouped the metrics around decisions, surfaced the important signals first and reduced the need to jump between disconnected views just to understand performance.

The challenge

Make website performance signals easier to inspect together.

What I changed

  • Group related metrics instead of presenting a wall of numbers.
  • Prioritize signals that can lead to an action.
  • Use visual hierarchy to separate overview from detail.
  • Keep the dashboard easy to scan repeatedly.

Where it ended

Performance can be reviewed from one clearer view, making it easier to spot what changed and what needs attention.
