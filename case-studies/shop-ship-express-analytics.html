The story behind the build

More data was never the goal. A clearer decision was.

Shop & Ship Express Analytics was about making website-performance signals easier to inspect together. A dashboard can show a lot of numbers and still fail if the person looking at it cannot tell what deserves attention.

I grouped related metrics around decisions, gave stronger signals more visual priority and reduced the need to move between disconnected views just to understand what changed.

The result is an operating view designed for repeated use, where the next question is easier to see.

The challenge

Make website performance signals easier to inspect together.

What I changed

  • Group related metrics instead of presenting a wall of numbers.
  • Prioritize signals that can lead to an action.
  • Use visual hierarchy to separate overview from detail.
  • Keep the dashboard easy to scan repeatedly.

Where it ended

Performance can be reviewed from one clearer view, making it easier to spot what changed and what needs attention.
