The story

What the project was really solving.

The project started with a familiar personal-brand problem: there was expertise to communicate, but the website still needed a simple story a first-time visitor could follow. Instead of treating every credential, service and idea as equally important, I shaped the page around recognition first. The person and expertise lead. Supporting information comes after. Contact stays visible without interrupting the story.

The challenge

Make expertise, services and the contact path easy to understand without turning the site into a dense biography.

What I changed

  • Keep the person and expertise central.
  • Group services around visitor intent instead of internal labels.
  • Use content hierarchy to make the professional story scannable.
  • Keep contact actions visible throughout the journey.

Where it ended

The finished experience gives visitors a clearer professional story and a natural route from understanding the person to starting a conversation.
