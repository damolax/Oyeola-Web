The story behind the build

The atmosphere mattered, but the website still had to explain what to do next.

Somatra Healing needed a softer, more reflective digital experience without allowing the service information to become vague.

I used Wix to keep the visual rhythm calm while making each service easier to understand in plain language. Booking and contact actions remain visible, but they do not overpower the atmosphere of the brand.

The result keeps the emotional quality of the wellness experience while giving visitors a practical route forward.
