Somatra Healing

A softer wellness-service experience built to preserve the brand atmosphere while keeping the practical next step clear.

Project context

The website needed to communicate a wellness-focused offer in a way that felt aligned with the brand while still giving visitors enough structure to understand the service and continue.

The friction

A calm visual atmosphere can become vague if the service explanation and action path are softened to the point that visitors cannot tell what to do.

Key design decisions

  • Use a calmer visual rhythm without reducing information hierarchy.
  • Explain the service before relying on atmosphere alone.
  • Keep relevant action points visible while preserving the wellness tone.

What changed in the journey

Visitors can take in the wellness positioning, understand the type of help offered and move toward the relevant next action without the page becoming vague.
