One Love Beauty

A beauty and hair-recovery website designed to make the service offer feel calmer, clearer and easier to act on.

What the business needed

The business needed a professional site where visitors could understand the main beauty and hair-recovery services, build confidence in the offer and reach the consultation or booking path without searching through the site.

What was built

The site was organized around a focused homepage, clearer service presentation, a simple explanation of the client journey, trust-building content and direct consultation and booking actions.

What is easier now

A visitor can move from understanding the service to seeing how the process works and choosing a consultation or booking action from one coherent journey.
