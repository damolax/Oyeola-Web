One Love Beauty

A beauty and hair-recovery website designed to make services, trust and the consultation or booking path feel calmer and more direct.

Project context

The business needed a professional site where visitors could understand the main beauty and hair-recovery services, build confidence in the offer and reach the consultation or booking path without searching through the site.

The friction

A booking calendar is not enough when a customer is still deciding which service fits, whether they need a consultation or what the experience involves.

Key design decisions

  • Make the service offer easy to scan before asking for a booking.
  • Explain the client journey so the next step feels predictable.
  • Place trust-building content and consultation actions where hesitation is most likely.

What changed in the journey

The customer can move from understanding the service to seeing how the process works and choosing a consultation or booking action from one coherent journey.
