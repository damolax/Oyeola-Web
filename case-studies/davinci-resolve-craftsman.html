The story behind the build

A specialist can have a lot to show and still make discovery difficult.

DaVinci Resolve Craftsman had specialist expertise and useful resources, but expertise only helps a first-time visitor when the website gives it structure.

I organised the experience around the specialty first, then grouped supporting resources by what someone was trying to learn or achieve. That stopped the site from feeling like an archive of disconnected material.

The result makes the specialist positioning easier to recognise and gives visitors a more deliberate route through the available knowledge.
