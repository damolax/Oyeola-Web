Real project · Creator / expertise website

DaVinci Resolve Craftsman

A creator-focused website built around specialist expertise, useful resources and clearer navigation.

Project context

The site needed to present specialist knowledge clearly and give visitors a practical way to move between the creator's expertise, resources and relevant next steps.

The friction

Specialist content can become an unclear mix of topics when expertise, resources and calls to action are not given separate jobs.

Key design decisions

  • Keep the specialist subject matter central to the visual hierarchy.
  • Organize resources around discoverable categories rather than a flat content list.
  • Use navigation that helps a visitor move from learning to the next relevant action.

What changed in the journey

The experience makes the expertise easy to identify and the resource path easier to continue through.
