The problem

The software may not be broken. The handoffs between it are.

Most businesses already have tools. The cost shows up when people become the connection between those tools.

What this looks like day to day

  • Managers ask for status updates that should already be visible.
  • The same client or project information is entered more than once.
  • Sales-to-delivery handoffs depend on someone remembering what to send.
  • Capacity problems become obvious only after the team is overloaded.
  • Reports take manual work and arrive after the decision window has passed.

What a better system gives you

  • One dependable view of status, ownership and next actions.
  • Information moves automatically where it should.
  • Managers see workload, blockers and delivery risk earlier.
  • Recurring reports are produced from live operational data.
  • The business depends less on one person remembering everything.
