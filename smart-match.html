      SMART FAQ

      Practical questions before you build.

      Can this work with my existing website?

      Yes. The implementation approach depends on your platform and how much logic, data and automation the experience needs.

      Does it require AI?

      No. Strong matching can be built with transparent rule-based logic. AI should only be added when it genuinely improves a defined part of the experience.

      Can the recommendation logic use my own rules?

      Yes. Your real service rules, eligibility criteria, product attributes, pricing behaviour and business constraints can drive the recommendation.

      Can prices update automatically?

      Yes, where your pricing data and platform support it. Fixed, tiered, calculated and indicative ranges can all be handled differently.

      Can it connect to my CRM?

      Yes. A completed match can pass structured fields into tools such as Airtable, Zoho, Pipedrive and other CRM or automation systems.

      Can customers receive their result by email?

      Yes. A live implementation can deliver a result or blueprint by email without forcing the customer to create an account.

      Can this work without customer accounts?

      Yes. The entire matching journey can work without authentication. Accounts are optional when cross-device saved history or private data is genuinely useful.

      Can I change questions myself?

      Yes. The engine can be connected to editable CMS or database content so questions, answers, weights and result copy can be managed without redesigning the interface.

      Can this work with hundreds of products?

      Yes, but the architecture changes. Product data, filters, scoring and inventory may need CMS, database or API integration rather than simple static rules.

      Can this integrate with booking?

      Yes. The recommendation can finish by opening the correct service, specialist, appointment type or booking path.

      Can Oyeola build only this system without redesigning my whole site?

      Yes. Smart Match can be introduced as a focused customer-journey feature when the rest of the website is already working well.
