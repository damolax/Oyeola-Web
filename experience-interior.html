Interior design concept

Restyle The Room

Let clients change materials and finishes directly on the room, not on a disconnected moodboard.

The customer problemMaterial swatches are difficult to judge in isolation. Clients need to see how walls, flooring, furniture and lighting work together.

What this solves

One interaction. A clearer customer decision.

This is a working concept, not a questionnaire disguised as a feature. The live interaction is the point: the customer changes something and the website responds in a way that helps them decide what to do next.

01

See combinations

Wall, floor, sofa and lighting selections update the same room in real time.

02

Compare directions

Clients can create a coherent version before asking the designer to develop it further.

03

Reduce revision friction

The designer receives a better sense of the client's visual preference from the beginning.
