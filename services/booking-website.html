For service businesses that need more bookings

Make the booking calendar the final step, not the first question.

Customers often need help choosing the service, understanding suitability, seeing proof or knowing what to prepare before they are ready to pick a time.

The friction

A booking button cannot solve uncertainty by itself.

If visitors do not know which service fits, what it costs, whether they qualify or what happens next, sending them straight to a calendar can create drop-off or poor-fit bookings.

The outcome

A pre-booking journey that prepares the right customer for the right appointment.
