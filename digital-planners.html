Why generic planners struggle

Page count is not a product strategy.

A planner becomes easier to choose when the buyer, workflow and navigation make sense as one system.

What makes it feel replaceable

  • The same monthly, weekly and daily pages buyers have seen everywhere.
  • Tabs and hyperlinks added after the layout.
  • Lots of pages without a clear job.
  • Listing images have to do all the differentiation.
  • The product competes mainly on style or price.

What makes it easier to choose

  • A clear buyer and use case.
  • Navigation designed as part of the product.
  • Pages that support a real workflow from start to finish.
  • Matching covers, stickers and presentation assets.
  • A product story that can be explained quickly.
