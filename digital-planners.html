The problem

A crowded marketplace makes generic products easy to replace.

If the product is mainly “pretty pages in a PDF,” buyers have little reason to choose it over dozens of similar listings.

What makes a planner feel generic

  • The same monthly, weekly and daily pages buyers have already seen.
  • Tabs and hyperlinks added after the layout instead of designed into it.
  • Too many pages without a clear buyer job.
  • Listing images have to do all the differentiation.
  • The product competes mostly on style, page count or price.

What makes the product easier to choose

  • A clear buyer and use case.
  • Navigation designed as part of the product.
  • Pages that support a real workflow from start to finish.
  • Matching covers, stickers and presentation.
  • A product story that can be explained quickly in the listing.
