How I work

Five clear stages. No jam-packed process.

The exact platform changes, but the thinking stays consistent from first question to final QA.

01

Understand

Clarify the audience, offer and the action the website should support.

02

Structure

Organize pages and information around how customers actually decide.

03

Design

Create the visual hierarchy, responsive layouts and proof placement.

04

Build

Develop the pages, forms, booking, ecommerce and interactions required.

05

Test

Check mobile, forms, links, accessibility basics and the conversion path.
