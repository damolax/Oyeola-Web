    FOR THE CUSTOMER

    “I understand what I’m building.”

    • I understand my options.
    • I know what affects price and timeline.
    • I can see what fits my budget.
    • I cannot accidentally keep an invalid combination.
    • I can compare versions and save my work.
    • I know what happens next.

    FOR THE BUSINESS

    Less repetitive qualification.

    • Fewer vague enquiries.
    • Better scope information.
    • More relevant enhancement opportunities.
    • Clearer customer expectations.
    • Faster quoting conversations.
    • Higher-intent enquiries.
