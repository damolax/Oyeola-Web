Fashion ecommerce concept

Live Outfit Studio

Let shoppers build a complete look on a model before they add anything to cart.

The customer problemA shopper likes one shirt but cannot picture the full outfit, colour balance or what to buy with it.

What this solves

One interaction. A clearer customer decision.

This is a working concept, not a questionnaire disguised as a feature. The live interaction is the point: the customer changes something and the website responds in a way that helps them decide what to do next.
