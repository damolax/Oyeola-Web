Website Check

Find out what is stopping more visitors from taking action.

The check looks at five things that affect whether a visitor can understand, trust and use your site: clarity, trust, conversion, discovery and technical foundation.

Where available, technical signals come from Google PageSpeed Insights / Lighthouse. If the public scan fails, those categories stay unscored rather than being invented.

Clarity

Can a new visitor tell what you offer, who it is for and why it matters from the first screen?

Conversion

Does the site help the right visitor take the next action without guessing?

Trust

Can visitors verify that you can deliver what you promise?
